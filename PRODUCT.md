# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to create or inspect QR codes quickly in a browser, including
Japanese- and English-speaking users on mobile and desktop. Their primary task
is to encode exact text or decode a local image without sending its contents to
a server.

## Product Purpose

Provide a lightweight, dependable QR workbench for generation, local PNG
download, and image decoding. Success means the encoded payload remains exact,
the generated code scans reliably, decoded content is presented safely as text,
and the full workflow stays understandable in either supported language.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, direct, and calm. The interface should feel technically capable
without distracting users from the QR task or overstating what the tool does.

## Anti-references

- Decorative spectacle that competes with QR readability or task completion.
- SaaS-style marketing chrome, fabricated proof, or unnecessary dashboards.
- Novel controls that replace familiar form, file-selection, and status
  patterns.
- Any experience that implies cloud upload, telemetry, persistence, or offline
  availability when those claims are not true.

## Design Principles

1. Correctness before styling: exact payloads and scan reliability are the
   product.
2. Keep processing visibly local and treat decoded content as untrusted text.
3. Make every state legible through clear labels, inline feedback, and familiar
   controls.
4. Keep Japanese and English behavior equivalent, not merely translated.
5. Preserve the lightweight static-site model and remove complexity that does
   not improve the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, programmatic labels and live status announcements,
keyboard-complete interaction, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced
motion, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, and feedback that does not rely on
color alone. Verify narrow viewports, long text, and both supported languages.
